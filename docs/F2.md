# F2 version 1: parity, homogeneity and derivative bounds

The target is F2, formulation version 1, in the Math 2.0 article
[Quaternions and Fueter regularity](https://z-amirkhosravi.github.io/homepage/math-2.0/quaternionic-analysis/foundations.html#f2).
The mathematical source is [KernelEstimates.lean](../MathResearch/Fueter/KernelEstimates.lean).

With `G(q) = conjugate(q) / ‖q‖⁴`, every real multi-index `α : Fin 4 → ℕ`
has a constant `Cα ≥ 0`, independent of `q` and `a`, such that for `q ≠ 0`
and real `a > 0`:

- `∂^α G(aq) = a^(-3-|α|) ∂^α G(q)`;
- `∂^α G(-q) = (-1)^(|α|+1) ∂^α G(q)`;
- `‖∂^α G(q)‖ ≤ Cα ‖q‖^(-3-|α|)`.

The closed declaration `MathResearch.Fueter.F2_v1` includes all three assertions,
oddness of `G`, evenness of each spatial derivative, and the explicit formula

```text
G_j(q) = -e_j / ‖q‖⁴ - (4 x_j / ‖q‖⁶) conjugate(q).
```

There are no unproved theorem parameters. The proof reuses F1's smoothness and
mathlib's real Fréchet chain rule, quaternion norm and conjugation laws,
compactness of the unit sphere, and boundedness of continuous functions on a
compact set. The intermediate homogeneity and parity results cover every finite
ordered list of coordinate derivatives; the multi-index theorems specialize them.

## Reading the statement

`multiIndexPartial` is the unchanged F1 definition: ordinary real coordinate
partials in the ordered basis `(1,i,j,k)`. The degree is `∑ μ, α μ`, with no
order bound. Reciprocal natural powers `(a ^ (3 + ∑ μ, α μ))⁻¹` represent the
negative integral powers in the article. Quaternionic multiplication by these
real scalars is written `•`.

`kernelSpatialPartial j` uses `j : Fin 3`; indices `0,1,2` correspond to the
article's spatial indices `1,2,3` through `j.succ`. The coordinate `x_j` is
`coordinateBasis.repr q j.succ`. The proved `inner_coordinateBasis` identifies
these coordinates with the corresponding real inner products.

All derivative claims are restricted to `q ≠ 0`; the ambient total definition
at zero is not used to claim differentiability there. Kernel oddness additionally
holds at zero. Requiring `Cα ≥ 0` strengthens the article's existential bound.

The formalization author checked this correspondence; this is not an independent
review of the translation. Proof validity and the meaning of the statement are
separate. Readers may inspect each definition and theorem without an approval
workflow.

and mathlib environment. This rebuilds every local module and audits all local
declarations, including private/generated declarations, for unfinished proofs
