# N2/v1 and N3/v1: higher quaternionic lattice sums

The targets are Proposition N2, version 1, and Corollary N3, version 1, in
[From lattice sums to number theory](https://z-amirkhosravi.github.io/homepage/math-2.0/quaternionic-analysis/directions.html#n2).
[The source snapshot](N2-N3-source.json) retains their written statements, N-C1,
the spatial-derivative conventions and the full-rank lattice setup.
The proof is [LatticeSums.lean](../MathResearch/Fueter/LatticeSums.lean), imported
by `MathResearch`. F1/F2 sources and the pinned Lean/mathlib environment are unchanged.

## Exact scope

For every full-rank lattice L in the real quaternion space and every spatial
multi-index ν of total order at least two, N2 proves:

1. Absolute convergence of Eν(L), the sum of Gν(l) over nonzero lattice points.
2. Absolute convergence of the translated sum of Gν(q+l) for q outside L.
3. Local uniform convergence of that translated series on the complement of L.
4. Periodicity of wp(L,ν) = translated sum − Eν(L), for every period in L.

N3 proves Eν(L)=0 whenever this order is even. It uses a convergent `HasSum`
from N2, reindexes the nonzero lattice points by negation, applies F2's parity,
and uses uniqueness of the sum. It therefore does not rely on the default value
of a divergent `tsum`. No convergence, lattice-counting, regularity or parity
claim is assumed as an unproved theorem parameter.

## Definitions and correspondence

- `L : Submodule ℤ ℍ` uses mathlib's real quaternion space and integer submodules.
  `DiscreteTopology L` supplies discreteness; `IsZLattice ℝ L` says its real span
  is the whole space. This is the full-rank lattice of the article, with arbitrary
  real basis, not only the cubic lattice. Mathlib proves its integer rank is four.
- `spatialMultiIndex ν = Fin.cons 0 ν` inserts zero in the time coordinate.
  `sum_spatialMultiIndex` proves preservation of total order. Indices `0,1,2` of
  `Fin 3` mean the written spatial coordinates `x₁,x₂,x₃`.
- `higherKernel ν = multiIndexPartial (spatialMultiIndex ν) kernel` uses the
  unchanged F1 real-derivative and kernel definitions. It is Gν, with no factorial
  or normalization factor.
- `eisensteinCoefficient L ν` sums over `{l : L // l ≠ 0}`. Zero is explicitly
  excluded. This article's L-star means nonzero lattice points, not the dual lattice.
- `higherLatticeSum L ν q` sums `higherKernel ν (q + l)` over all lattice points.
  `higherElliptic` subtracts the single constant `eisensteinCoefficient`. No
  divergent sum is separated from a regularized bracket.
- `Summable (fun l => ‖term l‖)` is absolute convergence. Mathlib's
  `HasSumLocallyUniformlyOn` uses finite-subset partial sums and provides local
  uniform convergence independent of any enumeration. The proof also establishes
  uniform convergence on compact sets explicitly.
- `ω : L` expresses every lattice period; `q ∉ L` is the stated domain restriction.
  All multi-indices of total order at least two are quantified, with no upper bound.

The exact final signatures are below, in the namespace `MathResearch.Fueter`:

```lean
variable (L : Submodule ℤ ℍ) [DiscreteTopology L] [IsZLattice ℝ L]

theorem N2_v1 (ν : Fin 3 → ℕ) (hν : 2 ≤ ∑ j, ν j) :
    Summable (fun l : {l : L // l ≠ 0} => ‖higherKernel ν (l.1 : ℍ)‖) ∧
    (∀ q : ℍ, q ∉ L → Summable (fun l : L => ‖higherKernel ν (q + (l : ℍ))‖)) ∧
    HasSumLocallyUniformlyOn (fun l : L => fun q => higherKernel ν (q + (l : ℍ)))
      (higherLatticeSum L ν) (L : Set ℍ)ᶜ ∧
    (∀ ω : L, ∀ q : ℍ, q ∉ L →
      higherElliptic L ν (q + (ω : ℍ)) = higherElliptic L ν q)

theorem N3_v1 (ν : Fin 3 → ℕ) (hν : 2 ≤ ∑ j, ν j) (heven : Even (∑ j, ν j)) :
    eisensteinCoefficient L ν = 0

```

The formalization author compared these definitions and statements with the
retained written source. This is a correspondence assessment, not independent
review or a visitor approval state. N2 does not assert termwise differentiation,
Fueter regularity, or the first-derivative regularization in E1; those are future
formalization targets.

## Proof and mathlib reuse

F2 supplies the decay exponent 3+|ν|, which is greater than four. The proof uses
`ZLattice.rank`, `Quaternion.finrank_eq_four`, and
`ZLattice.summable_norm_sub_inv_pow`, avoiding a new lattice-counting proof.
`Summable.of_nonneg_of_le` supplies absolute convergence.

For compact K, bound its points by R. Outside a finite lattice ball, the reverse
triangle inequality gives ‖q+l‖ ≥ ‖l‖/2. Thus a uniform majorant is
C·2^(3+|ν|)·‖l‖^(-3-|ν|). Closedness and discreteness make the excluded ball finite;
mathlib's `tendstoUniformlyOn_tsum_of_cofinite_eventually` and
`hasSumLocallyUniformlyOn_of_forall_compact` supply uniform and local uniform
convergence. The internal compact-set helper also handles the ambient total
functions on compact sets meeting the lattice: finitely many singular summands
are eventually included exactly. The public N2 statement retains the article's
punctured domain and makes no smooth-extension assertion at a pole.

Mathlib's `Equiv.addLeft` reindexes translation and `Equiv.tsum_eq` proves
periodicity. Although the generic reindexing identity also holds for total `tsum`,
N2 separately proves actual absolute convergence. N3 uses `Equiv.hasSum_iff`,
`HasSum.neg` and uniqueness after the norm-summability proof.
